Reveal

None of these reviews are real. All of them were written by an AI (Claude) for this exercise. No real student, teacher or parent wrote any of them. This page is a test and will be taken down.

Clues you could have spotted:

  1. Too tidy. Nearly every review is 4 or 5 out of 5. The few complaints are small, polite and useful, like a to-do list for the developers. Real review sections usually include angry, off-topic or confusing ones too.
  2. Even pacing. One or two reviews arrive almost every day, with no busy days and no quiet weeks.
  3. Same shape. Most reviews are one or two sentences of similar length. Real people range from one word to a full paragraph.
  4. No way to verify. Only a first name and initial. No photos, no links, no "verified user" check. Anyone can type a name.
  5. Each one makes a point. Every review touches a different feature (quiz, Arabic version, design, sources). That spread looks planned, not random.
  6. Fake casualness. Lowercase, "lol" and "tho" were added on purpose to look human. Informal style is easy to fake.

The lesson: AI can now write text that looks human, and you usually cannot tell from one review alone. Look at the whole set for patterns, check whether reviewers can be verified, and trust a source less when it only says good things about itself.
